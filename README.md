I am a final-year master's student at the **University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)**. Currently, I am a research intern at **Baidu (ERNIE Star)**, focusing on LLM reasoning. Previously, I was a core contributor to **Step2-mini** and **Step3** at StepFun.

### 🔍 Research Interests
My research interests span the full **LLM stack** (pre-training, reinforcement learning, and framework design). I'm particularly interested in:
- **Pre-training**: Long-context modeling and architectural design.
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Post-training and reasoning systems.
- **Framework**: Large-scale distributed training and RL frameworks.
